# Investigation User Flow Presentation

## Goal

Create a standalone HTML presentation that explains AI Intelligence as one continuous end-to-end investigation journey rather than as a feature catalogue.

## Approved content

The journey covers:

1. Starting a new investigation or continuing an existing one.
2. Exploring collections and examining individual objects in type-specific viewers.
3. Continuing interactive exploration or establishing a focused workstream.
4. Applying specialist-agent capabilities to create domain-specific analytical insights.
5. Creating structured knowledge and reports manually, through agents, or collaboratively.
6. Preserving knowledge, investigation memory, and workstream responsibility.
7. Evaluating new intelligence through broad investigation updates and focused workstream reassessment.
8. Creating a workstream alert only when a relevant change is significant enough to require attention.
9. Reviewing the result and returning to the investigation cycle.

## Content constraints

- Do not include implementation-status labels.
- Do not use a concrete operational scenario yet.
- Distinguish the personal assistant from specialist analytical agents.
- Present alerts as significance-based workstream events, not notifications for every new record.

## Acceptance criteria

- The complete journey is understandable from the overview alone.
- Every step also includes detailed explanatory text.
- Branching and convergence are visually explicit.
- The page is responsive and keyboard-navigable.
- The existing system-capabilities guide remains unchanged.
